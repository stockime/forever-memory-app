# The Baron Who Would Not Stay Down

I sold what I had carried out of Shadowfang's shadow to a merchant in Brill, bought little, and by the time I'd counted the coins the others had found me. Mira, Rakka, Sil and Juno. We didn't discuss it much. We went back down into the Ruins of Lordaeron.

I thought we had finished Baron Vardus two nights ago. He disagreed. We went down and put him on the floor among his stitched abominations and his necromancers. We climbed back up into the grey light of the Glades, came back to Brill, and went down again, and there he was. Then a third time. I have been raised once, and I remember what it cost. The Baron seems to get up as easily as a man rising from a chair. I don't know who does it for him, and I don't like not knowing.

Somewhere in the third descent the others went their own ways, as they did before. I stayed. Stubbornness again, I suppose, or the feeling that if I walked out he would simply go on making monsters out of our dead in the dark. So most of the day went down there, in the damp under the old capital. The necromancers kept at their chanting, their ghouls kept coming at me, and the abominations kept lurching out of the corridors, and I kept putting all of it down. The Baron rose more times than I care to write, and each time I met him with the shield.

I took a great deal of punishment today. I did not once ask the Light to close a wound. I'm not certain whether that was pride, or fear of asking and hearing nothing. I would like to believe it was pride. The shield held either way, and so did I, though my arm will argue the point tomorrow.

Among the wreckage I came away with a scepter, the Scepter of the Abandoned. It is a strange name for a thing to carry, and a strange thing for a Forsaken paladin to carry. I have not decided yet whether it is a joke at my expense.

Other travellers passed through the ruins while I was there, a handful of strangers going about their own grim errands. We nodded, mostly. Down there, that counts as fellowship.

I ended at Bandarion Keep and spoke with Danitha Morr. It was good to stand somewhere with walls that were ours.

Today's count is short, but I'll keep it: Mira, Rakka, Sil, Juno, walking out of the ruins again. Arugal still waits behind his walls, and the Wolfsbane Oath is still unkept. I spent the day on an old enemy instead of those. Tomorrow I turn south again.
