# What the Wolves Say About Us

Shari Stilwell greeted me for the third time with "Light keep you, Forsaken. Few would have believed it." I have decided it is a blessing she needs to hear herself say. She taught me more, I paid for it, and I went out feeling heavier in the arm and steadier on my feet.

Dalar Dawnweaver had a task that finally felt worth the shield. The worgen of Pyrewood, he said, "blame the Forsaken for every missing child. They are wrong. Correct them, and bring back the truth of what happens in that village at night." I have been accused of plenty that I did. It galls me to be accused of what I didn't do. On the way I cut through Dalaran's apprentices and their protectors, who had opinions about my passing.

Pyrewood killed me twice. A Darksoul put me down in the lane between the houses, and I came back out of the grey and walked straight into the same teeth. The second return was no easier than the first. I went back a third time out of stubbornness more than faith. After that the village went quieter, and I stayed on at its edges until the Whitescalps and Darksouls stopped coming. The Sepulcher gave me a thing called Pathfinder's Clearway and the usual promise that the Dark Lady will hear of it.

Then I went home to Brill, and for a while I was not alone. Mira, Rakka, Sil and Juno, all Bandarion like me, walked down with me into the Ruins of Lordaeron under Chancellor Amai's charge. There are necromancers down there who raise our dead again and again. I have been raised once. I do not care to watch it done to others like a chore. We broke their work, and then Baron Vardus's, the man who stitches abominations from the bodies of our people. Somewhere in that the others went their own ways and I finished it alone. Amai's people gave me the Baron's Signet. Last time it was a Scarlet's ring pulled from his hand. My collection of other men's rings is growing, and I don't like what that says about me.

Four walked out of the ruins alive. I won't claim I kept them that way, but I'll write their names down anyway. Mira, Rakka, Sil, Juno.

I ended the night beneath the walls of Shadowfang Keep. The Moonrage sentries and their gluttons kept coming out of the dark, and I kept putting them down. I asked the Light for very little today and it gave me very little, which is fair. What carried me was the shield.

Arugal is behind those walls. The Wolfsbane Oath is still unkept. I am closer to both than I was this morning.
